ReadyStart AI

A document-grounded construction handover and operational readiness assistant with human review required.

Launch ReadyStart AI ↗

Purpose and scope

Support project teams in reviewing evidence, actions and prerequisites for construction completion, commissioning, PSSR, operations training, maintenance readiness and turnover.

Responsible-use boundary

The assistant cannot approve energization, startup, isolation removal, work permit release, PSSR acceptance or final handover. Authorized human decision-makers retain these responsibilities.

The published prototype describes evidence/source/revision awareness and escalation of missing or conflicting safety-critical information. Displayed dashboard values may be illustrative.
