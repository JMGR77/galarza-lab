Data & interpretation

The snapshot contains 3,433 unique occurrences after the project’s documented preparation. Occurrence counts and proportions describe reporting records, not exposure-adjusted probability or operator risk.

Methods & outputs

The live project connects dataset preparation, dashboard exploration, twelve analytical questions, technical reporting and fifteen recommendations.

Operational boundary

The results support structured investigation and subject-matter review. They do not authorize process changes, pipeline integrity decisions or automated intervention.
