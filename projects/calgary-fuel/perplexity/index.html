Data preparation and evaluation

Sequential training: 2021-02-01 through 2025-08-29 (1,195 rows). Validation: 2025-09-01 through 2026-10-22 (299 rows), as recorded in the metadata file.

Predictors: price; price lags 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21; rolling mean and standard deviation (5); one-day percentage change; day of week; month. Features are standardized using training-only means and standard deviations supplied in metadata.

Target: target_next_day, meaning the next recorded price. Previous-price persistence is the documented baseline.

Limitations and responsible use

This upload contains no standalone inference model binary, application source, screenshot or public Perplexity project URL. The results can be inspected and downloaded, but new forecasts cannot be generated from this results page. MAE comparisons across AI platforms are not scientifically conclusive without the same holdout rows and evaluation protocol. Forecasts are estimates, not guaranteed retail prices.
