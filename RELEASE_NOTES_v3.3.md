## Local testing
Extract the entire ZIP, open the folder containing `index.html`, `assets`, `data` and `projects`, and double-click `Start_Galarza_Lab.bat` (requires Python) or run `py -m http.server 8000` in that directory. Open `http://localhost:8000/` and `http://localhost:8000/projects/fare-prediction/`. Avoid opening a directory in your browser with `file://`.
